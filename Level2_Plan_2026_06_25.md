# Level 2 Mechanics & Upgrades
Date: June 25, 2026

The goal is to increase the game pacing and depth for "Level 2" by adding new tiers of upgrades, dynamic payout scaling based on skill (boost catching, multi-catching), and introducing line-stretching mechanics.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. Level Selection (Dock Area)
- **Modifications:** Update `input.lua` and `ui.lua` to allow level switching while docked.
- **Impact:** Players can use the "Down" arrow in the dock area to toggle between Level 1 and Level 2. This will be fully unlocked for now to allow easy testing and switching back and forth.

### 2. Upgrade Screen UI Revamp (Progress Bars)
- **Modifications:** Redesign the layout in `ui.lua` to accommodate two rows of upgrades, and add visual progress pips for each upgrade.
- **Impact:** The screen will feature a row of 4 upgrades on top (Level 1) and a row of 4 upgrades below that (Level 2). Underneath each label, a small visual progress bar will be drawn to show completion at a glance (e.g., `[■■■□□□□□□□]`).

### 3. Level 2 Upgrades & Scaling (Prestige Tiers)
- **Modifications:** Expand `State.upgrades` in `state.lua` to enforce hard caps on Level 1 items and introduce Level 2 equivalents as "Prestige Tiers".
- **Level 1 Hard Caps:** 
  - Value, Speed, and Line max out at **Level 10**. `[■■■■■■■■■■]`
  - Boost maxes out at **Level 3**. `[■■■]`
- **Level 2 Upgrades ("Row 2"):**
  - **Value 2:** (Levels 11-20) Continues base payout multiplier. `[■□□□□□□□□□]` (Costs scale starting from Level 11 using base price of Value)
  - **Speed 2:** (Levels 11-20) Continues boat speed beyond the Level 1 cap. `[■□□□□□□□□□]` (Costs scale starting from Level 11 using base price of Speed)
  - **Line 2:** (Levels 11-20) Continues the maximum wake length further. `[■□□□□□□□□□]` (Costs scale starting from Level 11 using base price of Line)
  - **Boost 2:** (Levels 4-6) Unlocked in Level 2. `[■□□]` (Costs scale starting from Level 4 using base price of Boost)
  - *Note: All Level 2 upgrades (including Boost 2) are locked when on Level 1, and unlock immediately upon selecting Level 2.*

### 3b. Secret Menu Adjustments
- **Modifications:** Update the limits in `ui.lua` or `config.lua` for the secret debug menu.
- **Impact:** Increase the absolute maximums and minimums of the secret menu parameters by `+/- 10` to allow for wider manual tweaking during testing.

### 4. Bigger Fish (Level 2 Targets)
- **Modifications:** Expand the `State.fishSizes` table and logic to spawn rarer, larger fish variants once Level 2 is selected.
- **Impact:** These bigger fish will be faster and require larger loops (or perfectly timed boost catches) to capture, but they will yield exponentially higher base payouts to match the Level 2 upgrade costs.

### 5. Dynamic Wake Stretching (Further Boost)
- **Modifications:** Update the `maxPhysLen` calculation in `physics.lua`. 
- **Impact:** While `State.boat.boostFrames > 0`, the maximum physical length of the wake is multiplied by `1.10` (growing by 10%). When the boost ends, the bonus multiplier will gracefully decay back to 1.0 over the duration of the `boostCooldownFrames`. This causes the wake to slowly retract rather than snapping back instantly, making the mechanic feel fluid and providing a small grace period for loop closing.

### 6. Bonus Payout System (Skill Expression)
- **Modifications:** Update the `loop detection` block in `physics.lua` and the drawing logic in `main.lua` / `ui.lua`.
- **Impact:** 
  - **Boost Bonus:** If a loop is closed while the boat is boosting (or recently boosted), all fish caught in that loop receive a flat multiplier (e.g., 1.5x value).
  - **Multi-Catch Multipliers:** Catching more than 1 fish in a single loop applies a multiplier to the *entire loop's payout* (e.g., 2 fish = 1.5x, 3 fish = 2.0x, 4 fish = 2.5x).
  - **Visual Feedback:** We will add floating text or UI popups (e.g., "BOOST BONUS!" or "TRIPLE CATCH!") to make these skill plays feel incredibly satisfying.
